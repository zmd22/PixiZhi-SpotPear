# Changelog

## 1.1.4

Update the firmware first, then re-import the screens blueprint: the new
blueprint needs this firmware.

- Colour picker redrawn in the Soft LED look: 15 colours on the ring, 8 pastels
  and three whites. Swipe down or tap home to leave. The old picker's close
  button was blank.
- Player screen: small previous / play-pause / next controls you can tap; the
  one you used lights up, also for swipes and the double-tap.
- Text: curly quotes, dashes and ellipses no longer show as `?`, and Polish,
  Czech, Turkish and other Central European letters fold to their base letter.
- Planes only appear over the clock, not in the middle of a menu or while a
  finger is down, and no longer leave a stale song banner behind. Planes that
  have left the sky before the clock is free are dropped.
- Do Not Disturb also silences planes and the song banner; planes are skipped
  in night mode.
- Lists: tapping the V at the end of a list no longer makes the rows ignore taps.
- A timer that ran out more than ten minutes before the ball was switched on no
  longer rings at boot.
- Colour changes no longer end in NO CONFIRMATION when the light reports a
  slightly different colour back.
- With several balls, a status tap only runs the actions of its own automation;
  `esphome.pixizhi_timer_done` now carries `node`.
- Much less chatter: the blueprint sends a small keep-alive every 15 s instead
  of all screens, statuses and weather; data is sent when it changes, with a
  full resync every five minutes.
- Screens blueprint renamed "PixiZhi SpotPear - screens and controls".
- Internals: unused code removed, view numbers replaced by named constants,
  host tests and a CI workflow added, docs images regenerated by
  `tools/build_docs_images.py`.

## 1.1.3

Re-import the screens blueprint after updating: auto-brightness, the forecast,
the banner players and the radial menu all need it.

- Radial action menu replaces the pizza screen: hold the middle of the clock,
  slide to one of up to eight actions and let go (or let go in the middle and
  tap). Actions 1-4 keep their top/right/bottom/left places, 5-8 sit on the
  diagonals. Also opens from More > Actions. Set **Standby Long-press** to
  "Radial menu" (default on new installs); "HA action" and "Timer" still work.
- 12 new icons (unlock, blinds, camera, car, plug, thermometer, snowflake,
  shield, bolt, plant, scene, away); the blueprint icon lists now offer 40.
- Now-playing banner: a new song shows for 10 s with the artwork across the
  top half, scrolling title and artist, and the player name. Wakes the screen
  from standby; tap or swipe to dismiss. Hidden at night, in menus and during
  alerts. Pick several players in the blueprint, most important first; the
  banner follows the first one playing. **Now Playing Banner** switch to turn
  it off.
- Player screen redesigned like the banner, with the volume ring. In the lower
  half: swipe left for previous, right for next, double-tap to play/pause.
  Artwork dims while paused. Artwork Style (plain or pixelated) applies to both.
- Hourly forecast: tap the weather on the clock for the next five hours
  (temperature, icon, temperature bars and rain chance).
- Auto-brightness: choose a light sensor in the blueprint, then turn on
  **Auto Brightness** and set its minimum, maximum and full-brightness lux.
  Night mode keeps its own brightness.
- Plane screen: DEPARTING / ARRIVING tag with a small jet instead of the
  takeoff/landing pictograms.

## 1.1.2

- Fix the clock showing UTC: a configured fallback timezone disabled the
  timezone Home Assistant sends to the device. The ball now follows Home
  Assistant's timezone, including daylight saving (Home Assistant 2026.3+).
- Plane screen: several planes no longer get cut off by the screen timeout.
- Redrawn weather icons on the clock (11x9, larger): clearer sun, crescent moon,
  partly cloudy, slanted rain vs dotted snow, lightning, fog and wind.
- Faster charging detection (near-full voltage or a two-minute rise), so the
  battery ring hides sooner on the charger.
- "NO HA DATA" when Home Assistant is connected but the screens blueprint sends
  nothing (previously shown as "HA OFFLINE").
- Offline note on inner pages moved clear of the round edge; quick settings
  show --:-- until the clock is synced.

## 1.1.1

- Fix firmware updates: the update check failed on every install because
  GitHub's release downloads redirect to URLs longer than the default 512-byte
  HTTP buffers. Buffers are now 2 kB, and the manifest and OTA image are served
  from the GitHub Pages site (short URLs, no redirects).
- Devices on 1.0.0 or 1.1.0 cannot update over the air; flash 1.1.1 once over
  USB from the browser installer. Later versions install from Home Assistant.

## 1.1.0

- New "Soft LED" look on every screen: glowing round LEDs, one 5x7 font (no tiny
  3x5 text), unlit LED grid behind the clock digits, redrawn 9x9 and 11x11 icons.
- 20 fully saturated theme colours (`Theme Color`); status colours (battery,
  alarms) stay fixed, night mode still turns everything red.
- Quick settings: swipe down on the clock. Battery, Wi-Fi, HA link and time,
  plus brightness, ball volume, night mode, Do Not Disturb, audio output, timer.
- Do Not Disturb: normal notifications no longer light the LED and HA LED
  flashes are skipped; urgent alerts, timer and siren still sound.
- Sendspin album artwork on the player screen, as LED dots or the full image
  (`Artwork Style`).
- What's that plane: with `Plane Spotting` on, every new plane from the
  What's-that-plane integration is shown for 15 s (several planes cycle),
  with a takeoff icon for departures from `Home Airport` and a landing icon
  otherwise. Screens blueprint gains a "What's that plane" section.
- All drawing moved to `pixizhi_draw.h`; `tools/render_previews.cpp` renders
  the docs images with the same code.

## 1.0.0

- Public, credential-free firmware: Wi-Fi via the `PixiZhi Setup` hotspot with an
  on-screen QR code, API encryption key provisioned by Home Assistant, timezone
  received from Home Assistant. `secrets.yaml` is no longer needed.
- On-screen onboarding: setup QR, then IP and "add to Home Assistant" hint.
- Firmware updates from GitHub releases through the Home Assistant update entity
  (HTTPS with certificate verification, MD5-checked image). Network-push OTA is
  removed from the public build.
- Release workflow builds and attaches images and publishes a browser installer.
- Battery ring around the clock edge (`Battery Ring` switch), hidden while charging.
- `dashboard_import` for ESPHome "Take control".
- Release check entities from 0.9.9 replaced by the native update entity.
- Update screen with progress, "Restarting", or "FAILED / Old firmware kept".
- Automatic rollback: a new image must run for two minutes before it is
  confirmed; a crash or reset before then restores the previous firmware.
- Release notes (release title and text) shown in Home Assistant's update dialog.
- Siren: full-screen STOP button that works without Home Assistant, replays the
  built-in sound reliably without relying on media repeat, keeps the original
  volume when switched on twice, and hands the LED back to notifications.
- Siren always plays on the built-in speaker at full volume, even with the USB
  DAC selected; volume and mute are locked while it sounds, and the previous
  output and volume are restored afterwards.

## 0.9.9

- Kitchen timer counts from an absolute end time (no drift), survives reboots
  (running timers resume after time sync; expired ones ring), and exposes
  `Timer Ends` (timestamp) and `Timer State` entities to Home Assistant.
- Notifications persist across reboots and show their age ("12M AGO").
- Immediate HA/Wi-Fi offline indication on clock and dashboards; commands fail
  fast with "HA OFFLINE" instead of waiting for the 7 s confirmation timeout.
- Battery: readings are held during playback (speaker sag), new
  `Charging (estimated)` sensor from the idle voltage trend, configurable
  `Low Battery Warning` shown on the clock.
- Power: no frame work while the standby screen is black; optional
  `Deep Standby After` (off by default) with touch wake on the CST816 interrupt.
- Release check against GitHub (`Latest Release`, `Firmware Update Available`,
  `Check for Update`). TLS verification is now enabled for HTTP requests.
- Pizza screen supports up to three pages of four actions (swipe left/right);
  the screens blueprint adds a second page (slices 5–8).
- UI state constants and pure helpers moved to `pixizhi_ui.h`.

## 0.9.8

Initial public source release.

- Pixel-only clock and menus for home controls, notifications, timer and actions.
- Larger category/status text and vertical category navigation.
- Scheduled red night styling with independent saved brightness.
- Three Home Assistant blueprints, with no preselected household entity IDs.
- Internal/USB output selection, UAC 1.0 audio and USB software volume/mute.
- Paired-server Sendspin playback and on-device dynamic pairing code.
- Generic configuration, separate credential template, original font/sounds,
  preview images, installation guidance and third-party notices.

Requires ESPHome 2026.11.0-dev. Full UI/touch and sustained-playback verification
on physical hardware remains an early-release testing task.
